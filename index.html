Three different problems. Three different kinds of people.

Hand it enough, and the road splits. AI safety is not one problem with one kind of answer, and the three routes people worry about most need different training, different rooms, and different arguments.

Where we start: looking inside

Every one of those problems is easier to catch if you can see what a system is actually doing, not only what it says it did. That boat looked like it was winning.

Interpretability is the work of opening the network up and finding which parts respond to what, and why. It is the oldest question in the field, and the part of the problem this group starts with.

What we do

Fellowships

Weekly reading groups on the technical and policy sides of AI safety, from the fundamentals to current research.

Guest talks

Talks from researchers in AI safety and interpretability, from across Boston and beyond.

Research

Support for members pursuing their own AI safety research, with feedback and collaboration from other members and Northeastern faculty.

Who this is for

AI safety needs more than just AI researchers and software engineers. Many of its most important problems concern policy, law, cybersecurity, biology, and psychology. Anyone interested in making AI go well is welcome!
