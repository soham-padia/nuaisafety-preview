Fellowships

Starts January 2027 · Weekly, 90 minutes

We run two fellowships. Complete either one to become a fellow.

  1. Technical Fellowship

    An introduction to the technical side of AI safety: how modern AI systems are trained, why aligning them is difficult, and current research in areas such as mechanistic interpretability.

  2. Policy Fellowship

    An introduction to AI governance: the risks posed by advanced AI, how governments and companies are responding, and what effective policy could look like. No technical background required.

Sign up for the mailing list, and we’ll email you before the fellowships begin.

Executive team

Now recruiting · 3–5 hours a week

The executive team runs the group. We are looking for a few students to each take on one area this year.

  • Fellowships

    Organize the technical and policy fellowships.

  • Events

    Plan talks and socials.

  • Outreach

    Handle communications and bring in new members.

You do not need a background in AI safety or machine learning, and several roles are not technical. Expect 3–5 hours a week, including one team meeting. If you are on co-op, we will work around it.
